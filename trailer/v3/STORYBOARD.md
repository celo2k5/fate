# FATE — "The ring is the vault" (31 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps, VO + score)

## PAGE
- **P** People launching coins expect a pump; FATE's point is that the coin's economy is decided once and then runs itself. v2 trailer was rejected: text out of sync, too slow, weak transitions, weak character animation, ugly type.
- **A** Crypto-native viewers on X/TikTok, sound often on, skeptical of launchpad hype, 10–30 s attention.
- **G** Awareness → visit and launch a coin.
- **E** From "another launchpad" (bored, skeptical) to "oh — the coin does something by itself" (curious, a little impressed).
- **One true thing:** trading fees fill a per-coin vault, and when it reaches its threshold the destiny executes (Phoenix: buys back and burns). Real proof on screen: the Ember Finch coin page row "Bought and burned 3.60M EMBER with 0.3617 SOL on the curve".

## Ideas
1. (first, discarded) Coins flying up a rocket trail to "launch". Category costume.
2. UI documentary: one coin from picker → sign → trades → buyback row. True, but every launchpad film can be a UI tour.
3. **The ring is the vault.** The emblem's ring is the vault: fees fill it as an arc, and when the circle closes the destiny fires. The same ring travels through every destiny and closes back into the logo at the end.
**Pick 3**, carrying 2's real UI as its proof. Cover the logo and the film still says "a circle that fills and then acts" — that's FATE's mechanism, not a competitor's.
**Hero moment (screenshot):** the ember ring snapping shut around the glowing Phoenix, light flaring from the seam, cut a beat later to the real "Bought and burned 3.60M EMBER" row.

## World per act
- Act 1 (hook/turn): the crystal garden and the coin plate (site media), dim, key light from the ring itself. Sound: pad, voice.
- Act 2 (proof: choose): the real launch UI, magnified by the camera; dark ground of the site itself. Sound: kick enters, one UI click.
- Act 3 (hero): the Phoenix plate, the ring as light; the real coin page magnified. Sound: riser → **silence 0.45 s** → one impact as the ring closes.
- Act 4 (four destinies): each mascot plate, the ring in its colour, at a different place in frame each time. Sound: drop groove.
- Close: dark observatory plate, the emblem lands. Sound: groove drops out, pad + one low hit.
